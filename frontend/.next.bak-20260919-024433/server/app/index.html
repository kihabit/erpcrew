Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about KDS ERP Crew, AI agents, integrations, and getting started.

What is KDS ERP Crew?

KDS ERP Crew is an AI-powered platform that helps businesses automate workflows, create AI agents, and turn ERP data into actionable insights.

Can KDS ERP Crew work with my existing ERP?

Yes. It can work alongside your existing ERP systems without requiring you to replace your core ERP.

Can I create custom AI agents?

Yes. AI agents can be configured and customized around your specific business processes and workflows.

Can I use multiple AI/LLM models?

Yes. KDS ERP Crew provides flexibility to connect and use multiple AI/LLM models based on your business requirements.

Is my business data secure?

KDS ERP Crew is designed with enterprise-focused security and controlled access to help protect your business data.
